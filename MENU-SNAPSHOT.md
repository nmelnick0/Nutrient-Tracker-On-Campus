# Menu snapshot

Scope: 1901 Marketplace and Vista Grande, prioritizing recognizable main dishes and calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat. This narrower scope follows the student's September 12 request; it supersedes the earlier all-campus import target without changing the static-site or review guardrails.

Source: https://dineoncampus.com/calpoly/whats-on-the-menu

Menu date: September 11, 2026. Source panels were checked September 11–12; each dish carries its own check date and source URL. This is not live availability or a complete campus menu.

Nutrition is transcribed from the public menu's nutrition dialogs. Missing values, percent-only micronutrients, and non-exact quantities are not converted into invented amounts. Totals disclose incomplete coverage. No micronutrient estimates are used.

Main-dish name matches are sorted first for convenience; this is not an official menu category. All imported sides, drinks, and ingredients remain searchable. A component's portion is not a complete assembled meal: add each component actually eaten. Restaurants without imported items are hidden from the focused chooser. The original full location directory is retained in the data for future coverage work.

The daily spread is temporary and resets on reload. Every selected portion remains visibly listed with its source and check date and can be removed.
